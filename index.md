## Research Highlight

### MemeMatch: Dual-Context Multimodal Meme Dataset + Retrieval
MemeMatch is a large-scale multimodal meme dataset and retrieval system designed to study how meaning, intent, and emotion relate to online engagement and virality. It includes rich annotations such as **emotion vectors, topics, and usage-intent labels**, and is built with a dual-context pipeline that captures both:
- **Local context:** user overlay text (OCR) + post title
- **Global context:** underlying template semantics and visual meaning

On top of this representation, MemeMatch enables **intent-aware search** (e.g., “sarcastic memes about college”) and **image-based retrieval**, making it possible to trace how memes are reframed across communities and templates, and how those shifts correlate with engagement.
